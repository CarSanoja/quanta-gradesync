# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be decent. Assume the other person is trying to make the thing better.
Criticise the work, not the person doing it — and separate the two clearly enough
that the difference is obvious to a reader who is not you.

Report unacceptable behaviour to contact@somosquanta.com. Reports are handled
privately.
